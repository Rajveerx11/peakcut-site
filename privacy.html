PeakCut · Privacy · Terms

Privacy Policy

Last updated: 24 November 2026

PeakCut is a private tool used only by its owner to post short video clips to the owner's own channels.

What we access

When the owner connects a YouTube, Instagram or TikTok account, PeakCut receives a sign-in token from that service. It uses the token only to upload videos the owner has chosen and to read basic statistics (views, likes, comments) for those videos.

What we store

Sign-in tokens and video statistics are stored on the owner's own computer. They are not sold, shared or sent to anyone else.

Google user data

PeakCut's use of information received from Google APIs follows the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements. PeakCut also uses YouTube API Services; see the Google Privacy Policy.

Removing access

Access can be removed at any time from Google account permissions or the settings of the other services. Stored tokens are then deleted.
